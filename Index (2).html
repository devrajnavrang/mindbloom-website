The problem is growing

The lack of accessible mental health support among youth leads to stress, isolation, and anxiety. Too often, students and teens do not know where to turn.

😵

Stress overload

School, social pressure, and online life can create constant emotional strain without healthy outlets or guidance.

🤐

Silent suffering

Many young people hesitate to talk about how they feel due to stigma, fear, or lack of trusted adults.

💭

Anxiety escalation

Without early intervention, stress can grow into anxiety, sleep issues, panic, and reduced confidence.

Our solution

A safe, supportive digital space for youth mental wellness

This project creates an AI-powered chatbot designed for mental wellness check-ins, emotional support, and simple guidance when young people need someone to talk to.

✓
Daily emotional check-ins: Quick, friendly assessments that help students reflect on how they feel.
✓
Guided coping support: Practical exercises such as breathing, journaling, and grounding techniques.
✓
Encouragement & resources: Gentle prompts and pathways for further professional help when needed.
